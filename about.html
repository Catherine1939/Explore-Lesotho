About Lesotho

Lesotho is called the Kingdom in the Sky because it is entirely above 1,000 metres.

Why Katse?

Katse Dam is one of the biggest dams in Africa. Part of the Lesotho Highlands Water Project. Visitors come for boat rides, dam tours and mountain views.

  • Location: Thaba-Tseka
  • Height: 185m
  • Activities: Boat ride, Dam tour, Hiking

Capital: Maseru | Language: Sesotho & English | Currency: Loti (LSL)
